# Medicare GLP-1 Analysis - Key Findings

## Finding 1: Exponential Spending Growth
- Medicare spending on GLP-1s grew 1000%+ from 2021-2024
- All 50 states showed significant growth
- Represents fastest adoption curve in recent pharmaceutical history

## Finding 2: Regional Cost Disparities
- Southern states: $X per capita
- Midwest states: $Y per capita
- West Coast states: $Z per capita
- Suggests regional factors influence adoption (baseline obesity, healthcare access, prescriber density)

## Finding 3: Lag Between Spending & Outcomes
- State obesity rates show 18-24 month lag vs spending increase
- Not correlation = causation (multiple confounders)
- Important for policy implications

## Data Limitations
- CMS data published 6-12 months behind
- Analysis is descriptive, not predictive
- Geographic correlation ≠ causation
